Sources, definitions and what's left out
Census commuters
CSO Census 1986–2022 (FY079, F7065, E6011, FY080): people travelling to work, school or college, by usual means of travel. Working from home and "not stated" are excluded.
Commuter groups
CSO Census 1986–2022 (F7122): people usually resident and present in the State travelling to work, school or college, by means of travel and group (workers aged 15+, childcare 0–4, primary 5–12, secondary 13–18, students 19+). National figures only. "Not stated" is excluded from the shares; childcare was first counted in 2022.
Travel survey journeys
NTA National Travel Survey (NTA08, NTA10): share of all journeys by adults, by mode, degree of urbanisation and NUTS3 region.
Settlement types
Highly urban = EU "densely populated" areas or the five cities and suburbs; urban = "intermediate" areas or towns; rural = "thinly populated" areas or the remainder of the country.
Cars per 1,000
Private-tax-class vehicles under licence (TTA02) divided by population (CSO annual estimates, Census for earlier county years, interpolated between).
Public transport services
Estimate: weekday trips serving each area in the NTA GTFS timetable on a representative Wednesday. Each stop is placed in its Census 2022 Small Area, then divided by Census 2022 residents.
Person-km
Estimate: adults × journeys per adult per day (NTA04) × 365 × mode share (NTA08) × average journey length by mode, from the NTA distance bands with the open 8+ km band calibrated to the published average journey distance. It understates rail against the measured series because long rail trips fall in that open band.
Measured kilometres
Road vehicle-km by vehicle type from NCT odometer readings (THA10), scheduled bus vehicle-km (TOA16), rail passenger-km (TCA02).
Left out
Passenger satisfaction ratings (no source in the CSO folder); Northern Ireland stops in the GTFS feed; journeys by people under 18 in the travel survey figures.

Source: Central Statistics Office (CSO) PxStat and National Transport Authority open data, used under CC BY 4.0.
